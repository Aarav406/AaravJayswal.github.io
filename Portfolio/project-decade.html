Decade Counter Display - Fundamental of Digital Circuits

CD4511BE SN74LS90 NE555P K-MAP 7-segment Display PCB Designing

Project Details

  • Designed and fabricated a PCB implementing a 7490 decade counter driven by a 555 timer (astable); frequency adjustable via potentiometer to vary counting speed.
  • Routed Clock A to QA to configure the 7490's internal JK flip-flops for mode-10 counting and added a pull-down resistor to prevent QA from floating, restoring accurate decade counting.
