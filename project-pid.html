PID Temperature Controller & Custom Power Supply (Buck Converter)

PSpice Timer C PID Control

Project Details

  • Implemented a closed-loop thermal management system on an Arduino, driving a DC fan via PWM based on continuous PID calculations from DHT11 feedback.
  • Integrated a custom 10V-40V to 5V buck converter to power the logic circuitry, utilizing an LM2576 and LC filter to minimize output voltage ripple.
